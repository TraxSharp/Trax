// TraxBanClock: the clock is an input. Code that reads it cannot be replayed or tested at a chosen
// time. Take a TimeProvider and ask it. TimeProvider.System stays allowed: the composition root has
// to name the real clock somewhere, and a ban with no way out would only push it into a subclass.
P:System.DateTime.Now;Take a TimeProvider and call GetLocalNow()
P:System.DateTime.UtcNow;Take a TimeProvider and call GetUtcNow()
P:System.DateTime.Today;Take a TimeProvider and call GetLocalNow()
P:System.DateTimeOffset.Now;Take a TimeProvider and call GetLocalNow()
P:System.DateTimeOffset.UtcNow;Take a TimeProvider and call GetUtcNow()
P:System.Environment.TickCount;Take a TimeProvider and call GetTimestamp()
P:System.Environment.TickCount64;Take a TimeProvider and call GetTimestamp()
M:System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch.GetTimestamp;Take a TimeProvider and call GetTimestamp()
M:System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch.StartNew;Take a TimeProvider and call GetTimestamp(), then GetElapsedTime()
M:System.Diagnostics.Stopwatch.#ctor;Take a TimeProvider and call GetTimestamp(), then GetElapsedTime()
